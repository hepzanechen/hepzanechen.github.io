Some questions need a piece of code. Some need a diagram. Others need a clearer statement of the question itself.

This blog is a place to document that process: computational physics, AI tools, and research methods, alongside unresolved questions, provisional judgments, and ideas that change with new evidence.

## What belongs here

**Physics and computation.** Notes that connect model assumptions to numerical implementations, showing how a result is obtained and the conditions under which it holds.

**Tools and practice.** Reusable approaches to programming, automation, and AI-assisted work, with links to public code whenever possible.

**Research notes.** Connections between concepts, including the parts that remain unresolved. A small question is enough to begin a useful note.

## Why two languages

Chinese offers a natural space for organizing thoughts. English makes those ideas accessible to people from other backgrounds. Each article has its own Chinese and English pages; the language button in the header takes you to the corresponding version.

The versions should communicate the same ideas without requiring a word-for-word translation. What matters is preserving the reasoning, the conditions of a claim, and the uncertainty that remains.

## Begin with a small question

A useful record does not need a grand conclusion. It can start with three questions:

> What am I trying to understand? What evidence do I have? What next step would make the question clearer?

That is where this blog begins.
